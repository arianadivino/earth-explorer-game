    How to Play the Game

    1. Select a Feature on the Earth

    Click on Mountain, Sea, Forest, or Volcano on the rotating Earth.

    2. Read the Information Panel

    Learn the definition, explanation, and interesting facts about each feature.

    3. Learn How Features Form

    Understand the processes that create these natural features.

    4. Discover Connected Disasters

    See which natural disasters are related to each feature.

    5. Explore Different Parts of Earth

    Visit all features to complete your discovery log.

    6. Select Your Character

    Choose a character (Explorer, Scientist, Geologist, or Ranger) that matches your learning style.
